How We Train

Short sessions. Serious output.

Every class runs 45 minutes flat — warm-up to cash-out — because that's the length our coaches found people actually finish without cutting corners.

Veyronix Fitness was started by three coaches who'd all worked at gyms where the real coaching happened in the first two weeks and then quietly stopped. Here, every session is still led live, every lift is still watched, and your programming still changes as you get stronger — six months in, not just six days.

We run four tracks — strength, conditioning, boxing, and recovery — and most members rotate through more than one.

What We Run

Four tracks. One standard.

Pick a lane or rotate through all four — every track shares the same warm-up floor and coaching standard.

Power Lab

Barbell strength and hypertrophy work. Squat, bench, and deadlift progressions with real periodization, tracked week to week.

Mon / Wed / Fri / Sat — 45 min

Engine Room

Sled pushes, intervals, and metabolic circuits built to raise your ceiling for everything else you do. High output, no ego.

Tue / Thu / Sun — 40 min

Knockout

Technical boxing fundamentals, pad rounds, and bag work led by a former amateur competitor. First class is footwork only — no pressure.

Mon / Wed / Fri — 50 min

Reset

Mobility flows and soft-tissue work so the other three tracks don't wreck you. Included free with every membership tier.

Daily — Open Access
